Quadratic Equations

Mathematics SS2 schedule 40 mins

flag Learning Objectives

  • Define a quadratic equation and identify its standard form (ax² + bx + c = 0).
  • Solve simple quadratic equations using the factorization method.
  • Apply quadratic equations to solve basic real-world word problems.

directions_run Teaching Activities

5 mins
Introduction: Briefly review linear equations. Introduce the concept of a variable squared using area of a square example.
15 mins
Direct Instruction: Write the standard form on the board. Demonstrate factorization with a clear example (e.g., x² + 5x + 6 = 0). Show how to find factors of 'c' that add up to 'b'.
15 mins
Guided Practice: Have students pair up and solve three varying problems on the board or in their notebooks while the teacher circulates to offer support.
5 mins
Conclusion: Recap the steps for factorization. Assign homework from the textbook (Exercise 4.2, Q1-5).

assignment_turned_in Assessment Strategy

Formative assessment during guided practice by checking students' steps in factorization. A quick 2-question exit ticket at the end of the class asking them to factorize a simple expression to gauge immediate understanding.
