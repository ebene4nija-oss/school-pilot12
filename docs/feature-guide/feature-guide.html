01The platform at a glance

What it is, and how it thinks

SchoolPilot runs teaching, learning, administration, boarding, communication and examinations for private K-12 schools of 50 to 3,000 students, schools currently running on paper, Excel or another portal. It is software-only: no biometric readers, no RFID, no scanners, no sensors. Attendance uses the phone camera already in a teacher's pocket, staff check in with their own phone's GPS, and the bus is tracked from the driver's phone.

Six commitments behind every feature

1 · Software-only, always

If it can't run on a phone camera, phone GPS or a browser, it doesn't ship. Hardware is a permanent boundary, not a later phase.

2 · Nigerian K-12, not translated

Sessions, terms, classes and arms from nursery to SS3. CA schemes, broadsheets, WAEC. No faculties, no semesters, no GPA.

3 · Works when the network doesn't

Registers and scores queue on the phone with no signal and sync safely; CBT runs in the lab with no internet.

4 · AI drafts, teachers decide

Every AI-drafted remark waits for a teacher to approve or edit it. A report card cannot carry an unapproved comment.

5 · NDPA-first, because the records are children's

Data minimisation by default: a child's name is never sent to an AI provider, and medical fields are encrypted.

6 · Your records stay yours

Free migration in, a full export out, whenever you ask. Nothing about leaving is designed to be difficult.

02Running the school

Records, structure & the daily loop

Foundation

Your own school portal LIVE

Every school gets its own web address, its own branded sign-in page and fully isolated data: no other school can see a record of yours. We migrate your existing records in, free, within 24 hours.

Users, roles & permissions LIVE

Create accounts, reset passwords and lock accounts from one screen. Custom roles for schools that split responsibilities differently: a bursar who sees finance but not medical notes.

Student records & lifecycle LIVE

The Nigerian registration form, digitised, with several guardians per child for split families. Promotion, repeat, withdrawal, graduation and transfer are kept as records, never overwritten, and duplicate applicants are flagged.

Admissions LIVE

Parents apply online with documents; review, entrance assessment, offer and enrolment create the student record with no retyping.

Academic history & transfers LIVE

Each term is closed into a permanent record. Transcripts and transfer certificates carry a QR code to verify them; records received from another school keep their original names beside yours. Families can report a mistake for the school to correct.

Staff, HR & payroll LIVE

Staff records, contracts, class and subject assignment, leave requests, loans and monthly payroll with payslips, approved before it is paid.

The daily loop

Attendance LIVE

By QR scan through the app's own camera or by register, with corrections approved and an alert to parents of absent children. Staff clock in with their own phone's GPS, checked against the school's gate.

Timetable LIVE

A conflict-free master timetable generated around teacher availability, saved as versions. Students see theirs as a calendar they can print.

Cover & leave LIVE

Teacher leave requests, approval, and cover for the lessons they miss, so a class is never left without a teacher.

03Assessment, results & CBT

The module a school feels first

Result processing is where the manual alternative hurts most. Days of a form teacher's time become a background job, and the report card that comes out can be verified as genuine by anyone holding it.

Results

CA schemes per section LIVE

Number of CAs, their weights and grade boundaries are set per section: nursery, primary, JSS and SS can each grade differently. Homework and coursework can fill a CA, adjusted by the teacher.

Broadsheets & positions LIVE

Totals, averages, class and subject positions computed as scores arrive; termly and annual broadsheets; changes after submission go through approval with the history kept.

Your report card design LIVE

Choose a template carrying your crest and sections. Nursery classes can use developmental ratings instead of scores.

QR authenticity LIVE

Every report card carries a QR code. A parent, a receiving school or an employer can scan it and confirm it is genuine and unaltered.

Result PINs LIVE

Issue result-checker PINs in batches and track their use: the scratch-card model parents already know, issued digitally.

You decide when results go out LIVE

Results reach families only when the school releases them for a term, so a half-entered broadsheet never leaks out as a result.

Computer-based testing

CBT engine LIVE

WAEC, NECO and BECE-style papers: question and option shuffling per candidate, timed sittings with autosave, automatic objective marking, pass marks.

  • Comprehension passages with several questions
  • Maths rendered without internet
  • Theory answers marked by the teacher

Question bank LIVE

Tagged by subject, topic and difficulty, with bulk import and versions kept when a question is edited, reusable across exams and terms.

  • Images in questions and options
  • Per-topic score breakdown

Lab client, no internet PILOT

One school laptop downloads the sealed paper the day before and serves it to the lab PCs over the lab's own network, wired, Wi-Fi or its own hotspot. Answers upload afterwards; a PC that dies resumes where it stopped. In pilot with schools now.

An AI comment never reaches a report card unreviewed

SchoolPilot can draft a remark from a student's subject and score, but every draft lands in pending approval and stays there until a teacher approves or edits it. The report card generator refuses to print an unapproved comment. The child's name is added on our side; it is never sent to the AI provider.

04Teaching, AI & money

The classroom and the bursary

Learning

Materials & homework LIVE

Notes, slides and worksheets per subject, reviewed before students see them. Homework submitted online, marked with feedback, and counted towards CA.

Virtual classes LIVE

Lessons scheduled on the school's Zoom or Google Meet, with attendance read from the meeting report.

Progress & points LIVE

Per-topic mastery from tests and tutor sessions, points and badges, and a progress view for each student and their parents.

AI, where it earns its keep

AI Studio for teachers LIVE

Lesson plans with objectives, activities and assessment; exam questions and homework ideas; translation; and performance summaries drafted from a student's released results. Every draft is for the teacher to edit, on the Nigerian curriculum.

AI tutor for students LIVE

A study tutor that guides rather than hands over answers. The school sets which sections and subjects may use it, a daily message limit, and a lock during CBT exams. Conversations are kept so a student picks up where they left off.

Your choice of AI NEW

Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini: the school chooses the provider and a quality level (fast, balanced or best), and can switch at any time from its AI settings.

Every AI feature has an off switch LIVE

Report comments, lesson plans, exam questions, homework ideas, translation, summaries and the tutor are each their own switch. A daily spending limit applies to every school.

Fees, finance & accounting

Fee structures & invoicing LIVE

Fees per class and term drive the term's invoices automatically, with instalments that must add up to the balance, scholarships and discounts, and a list of who owes.

Collecting payments LIVE

Paystack and Flutterwave for online payment, configured per school; cash and transfers recorded at the bursary on the same ledger, each with a printable receipt.

School accounting LIVE

Expenses, vendors, petty cash, budgets and payroll, so the bursary runs in the same system as the classroom instead of beside it in Excel.

Fee reminders that don't harass LIVE

Reminders to parents with a balance, deliberately throttled. Push is free and SMS is billed, so the channel is always the bursar's choice.

05Boarding, campus & families

Hostel, transport, library, health & parents

Boarding

Hostel management LIVE

Houses, rooms and beds; boarding applications and allocation for full, weekly and flexible boarders; hostel assets and maintenance requests.

Exeats, roll calls & movements LIVE

Leave requests approved with the parent's consent, roll calls, sign-out and return logged with late returns flagged, and incident reports.

Hostel fees on the invoice LIVE

Boarding fee rules per category, billed onto each boarder's term invoice, so they are paid through the same ledger as school fees.

Sick bay LIVE

A clinic log of visits and treatment, with medical fields encrypted and visible only to the roles that need them.

Around the campus

Transport LIVE

Buses and routes, with the bus's position shared from the driver's phone while a route runs.

Library LIVE

Catalogue, borrowing and returns by phone-camera barcode, and each student's books on loan.

ID cards LIVE

Card designs per school, print-ready PDFs with a QR code, reissue and revocation of lost cards.

Families & communication

Parent portal & app LIVE

Each child's attendance, homework, results, fees, hostel and ID card. In split families each guardian gets their own access to the same child.

Safe pickup & consent LIVE

Parents name who may collect their child and can withdraw it at any time. Consent, including for AI features that use a provider outside Nigeria, is the parent's choice, never assumed.

Messaging & notifications LIVE

A staff inbox, announcements, push notifications to the app, and SMS and WhatsApp for messages that must reach a feature phone.

Behaviour & portfolio LIVE

Behaviour notes that reach the real guardians, and a portfolio of work and achievements beside the marks.

No biometrics is a compliance decision, not only a cost one

Biometric data is a stricter category of sensitive data under the NDPA. By never collecting it, your school avoids a whole tier of consent and security obligations, on top of the hardware saving.
